Privacy Policy — AIClothesRemover

Last updated: 18 September 2026

What we collect

Uploaded images, the text prompts you submit, basic account data (email, plan, quota) and standard server logs including IP address and user agent.

How we use it

To process your edit, enforce the acceptable use policy, prevent abuse, and operate billing. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your uploads to train models without your explicit opt-in.

Retention

Uploaded images are deleted automatically within 24 hours. Prompts and generated outputs are deleted within 30 days unless you save them to your account. Server logs are retained for 90 days for security and abuse investigation.

Processors

We use third-party infrastructure for hosting, model inference, payments, email and analytics. Each is bound by a data processing agreement and only receives the data needed to perform its function.

Your rights

Under GDPR and CCPA you can request access, correction, deletion, portability, and object to processing. Email the address on the report page and we respond within 30 days.

International transfers

Data may be processed outside your country. Transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Children

The service is for adults. Images depicting minors are rejected and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
