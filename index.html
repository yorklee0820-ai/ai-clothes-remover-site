AI clothes remover FAQ

What is an AI clothes remover?
It is a browser tool that removes or replaces garments in a photo using AI — jackets, coats, scarves or a whole outfit — while keeping the person, pose and background unchanged.
Is there a free AI clothes remover?
Yes. You get three free edits per month with no signup, no card and no watermark on the preview. Full-resolution downloads start with Pro.
Can I use it online without signing up?
Yes. The first edits run entirely in the browser — upload a photo, describe the layer and download the result. A free account is only needed to save history.
Can it remove a jacket from a photo?
Yes. Jackets, coats and scarves are the most reliable cases. Name the garment and its colour, and state what should stay visible underneath.
Can it change clothes in a photo?
Yes. You can replace one visible outfit with another by describing fabric, colour and fit. Use the AI clothes changer for full outfit swaps.
Does it work on mobile? Is there an app?
Yes. The tool runs in any mobile browser on iOS and Android — no install required. The layout is built mobile-first.
Will the face and body stay the same?
Yes. Identity, body shape, pose, lighting and background are locked by default, so only the selected garment changes.
What photo formats are supported?
JPG, PNG and WEBP up to 12 MB. Higher-contrast photos with a clearly separated garment give the cleanest results.
Is it legal to remove clothes from a photo?
Yes, when you own the photo or have permission to edit it, the subject is an adult who consented, and the result stays non-sexual. Uploads are auto-deleted within 24 hours.
What images are not allowed?
Images of minors, non-consensual or private photos, and any request for nudity or sexualised output. Such uploads are blocked and accounts are closed.
