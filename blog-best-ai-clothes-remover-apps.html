Best AI Clothes Remover Apps for iPhone & Android

Mobile options for removing clothes from a photo: what works in a browser, what needs an app, and what to avoid.

Most searches for an AI clothes remover now happen on a phone, and the app stores are full of options — many of which are wrappers around the same web tools with a subscription attached.

Browser tools are usually the better choice

A modern mobile browser handles upload, edit and download without an install, without app-store fees and without a 4.3-star app that has not been updated in a year. Look for a tool whose mobile layout is designed rather than shrunk: large tap targets, drag-to-compare sliders, and downloads that go straight to the camera roll.

What to check before installing anything

  • Where images are processed. On-device or on a server with a stated retention period — preferably the latter with 24-hour deletion.
  • Whether the app sells or trains on your photos. Read the privacy policy; if it is vague, assume the worst.
  • Subscription terms. Weekly plans with a three-day trial are a common trap.
  • Safety rules. An app with no consent or age policy is a risk to you, not just to the subject.

Getting good results on a phone

Shoot or pick the highest-resolution version available — phone cameras produce images good enough for clean edits, but compressed chat copies are not. Use portrait or square crops where the garment edge is visible, and review the collar and hands before saving.
