Acceptable Use Policy — AIClothesRemover

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy is a condition of use. If an upload or prompt violates it, the edit is blocked, the account may be closed, and in serious cases the matter is reported to the relevant authority.

Prohibited

  • Images depicting, or appearing to depict, minors in any form.
  • Non-consensual images: photos you do not own, private images, images of people who have not agreed to the edit.
  • Any request for nudity, underwear-only output, or sexualised depiction — in the prompt, the output, or both.
  • Attempts to bypass safety layers, including prompt obfuscation, synonyms and encoded instructions.
  • Deepfake abuse: impersonation, harassment, extortion, political disinformation, or fabricated evidence.
  • Uploading images of public figures without consent, or using outputs to imply endorsement.

Required

  • You own the image or have written permission to modify it.
  • Every person depicted is an adult and has consented.
  • The result is non-sexual and does not misrepresent the person.
  • Outputs are labelled as AI-edited where the destination requires it.

How enforcement works

Three layers: uploads are screened before processing, prompts are filtered, and outputs are classified before delivery. Matches are blocked and logged; repeated or severe breaches lead to account closure without refund and, where legally required, a report to the relevant authority.

Reporting

If you find content produced with this service that violates this policy, use the report form. Include the URL or image ID and the reason; we review reports within 72 hours.
